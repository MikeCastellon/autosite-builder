# Google's rules for this deck

The copy is pasted into the customer's Google Business Profile and used to ask for reviews, so it has to
follow Google's policies. The validator checks what it can; the rest is on you.

## Business description

- Up to 750 characters, about the business itself: what it does, where, what sets it apart.
- No links or HTML, no phone numbers needed (the profile shows the phone), no special offers, prices or
  sales, no misleading or unverifiable claims, nothing about other businesses.

## Categories

- One primary category (the one that best describes the core business) and a few additional ones for real,
  distinct services. Fewer and accurate beats many.
- Google's category list changes; only names close to GBP's are useful, and the owner must pick the exact
  name from the GBP category box. Every category we suggest carries `"confirm": true`.

## Posts

- Up to 1,500 characters. A phone number in the text gets the post rejected: use the CALL button.
- One button per post: Book, Call now or Learn more (the Book and Learn more buttons need a link; the server
  adds the site's or the booking page's).
- Photos must be the business's own (their work, van, shop, team). No stock or generated "our work" images,
  no photos that are mostly text.
- No misleading content, no prices that aren't real, no claims the business can't back.

## Asking for reviews

- Ask every customer, the same way. Asking only customers you expect to be happy ("review gating"), or
  sending unhappy ones somewhere else, is not allowed.
- Never offer or hint at anything in return: no discounts, gifts, free services, entries to win, cash or
  credit. Not even "as a thank-you".
- Never ask for a particular rating or kind of review ("leave us 5 stars", "if you were happy").
- Never write the review for the customer or post reviews from staff or family.

## Replying to reviews

- Reply as the business, politely, without arguing or sharing private details of the customer or the job.
- Never offer something to change or remove a review.
- For complaints, invite the customer to get in touch (the business phone, or reply) so it can be put right.
